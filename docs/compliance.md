# Compliance

Certeverin is designed for permissioned collection.

- Use official APIs where available.
- Do not build direct LinkedIn scraping.
- Respect robots.txt, site terms, rate limits, caching, and attribution.
- Store full descriptions locally for analysis but do not republish them in reports.
- Export aggregate statistics, snippets, counts, and source URLs.
- Keep development sample data out of production searches unless explicitly requested through the API.
- Include sample-size, source-mix, recency, and confidence limitations in the PDF.
